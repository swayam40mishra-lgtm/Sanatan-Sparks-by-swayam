My Jaan,

I know you've carried so much on your own.

So many battles, so many tears, so many nights where you had no choice but to be strong because nobody was there to hold your hand.

But I want you to know something.

You don't have to carry everything alone anymore.

From now on, whatever comes — the good days, the bad days, the victories, the breakdowns, the fears, the storms — let's face them together.

I know trust is not easy for you.

Life has given you enough reasons to be careful.

And I don't expect you to trust me just because I ask you to.

Trust is built through actions, not words.

That's why all I can do is keep showing you through my actions every single day.

Whether you trust me or not is your choice, and I will respect that.

But my responsibility is to keep proving that I am worthy of that trust.

I may not be the most handsome man in the world.

I may not be perfect.

I may not always have the right words.

But there is one promise I can make with complete honesty:

I will never intentionally hurt you.
I will never cheat on you.
I will never betray you.

And I will never give you a reason to doubt my character or question my loyalty.

Not in front of you.

Not behind you.

Beside you. Always.

Out of billions of people...

Somehow...

I found you.

And I would choose you again.

Every single time.
